About Sababehpar Iranian

From mine to market — pioneer in high-purity calcium carbonate and micronized mineral processing from Shaghez Aligoodarz quarries.

01

Our History & Vertical Integration

Founded in 2010, Sababehpar Iranian Mining Company operates directly from raw marble extraction at our owned quarry in Shaghez, Aligoodarz to micronized powder packaging at our advanced 3-line processing facility.

Our Aligoodarz deposits are recognized globally for their exceptional natural whiteness (98%+) and high calcium carbonate purity (99%+ CaCO3), serving downstream polymer, coating, dry-mix mortar, and paper manufacturers.
