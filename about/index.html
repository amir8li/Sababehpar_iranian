SABABEHPAR IRANIAN MINING CO.

Pioneering Industrial Minerals & Powder Innovation

From raw marble quarry extraction in Shaghez Aligoodarz to high-precision ball mill micronizing, Sababehpar Iranian delivers pure calcium carbonate and talc powders to global B2B manufacturers.

01

History & Company Foundations

1. Founding & Mining Rights (2010): Sababehpar Iranian Mining Company was established in 2010 after acquiring direct mining extraction rights at the world-renowned Shaghez marble deposits in Aligoodarz, Lorestan province.

2. Vertical Integration Strategy: Unlike standard processing trading firms, Sababehpar operates fully vertically integrated operations — controlling raw quarry block selection, crushing, continuous ball mill micronizing, and automated packaging under one roof.

3. Exceptional Chemical Purity (99%+ CaCO3): Aligoodarz marble is internationally recognized for its crystalline structure and ultra-high chemical purity, yielding natural whiteness indices exceeding 98.0% with minimal silica impurities.

4. Automated Micronizing Lines & Coating: Our Aligoodarz processing plant operates 3 automated production lines, including specialized high-temperature stearic acid surface treatment units for polymer extrusion grades.

5. Scaled B2B Capacity (150,000+ Tons): With an annual processing capacity exceeding 150,000 metric tons, Sababehpar serves domestic and regional polymer, coating, masterbatch, mortar, and paper manufacturing industries.

03

Sustainability & ESG Commitment

1. Closed-Loop Water Recycling (95%+): All processing plant wash water is directed into closed-loop clarifier tanks, recycling over 95% of industrial water with zero chemical effluent discharge into local watersheds.

2. Energy Efficient Micronizing (22% Savings): By installing frequency variable drives on our main ball mill motors, Sababehpar has reduced electricity consumption per metric ton of processed powder by 22%.

3. Progressive Quarry Land Restoration: Wire-saw extraction techniques eliminate explosive blasting damage, allowing progressive backfilling and topsoil revegetation at Shaghez mining sites.

4. Dust Containment & ISO 14001 Standards: Fully enclosed pneumatic conveying lines and pulse-jet bag filter houses maintain workplace dust emissions below 5 mg/m³ in compliance with ISO 14001 environmental standards.

5. Alignment with United Nations SDGs: Sababehpar aligns its corporate social responsibility roadmap with UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs 6, 7, 12, and 13) for sustainable industrial production.
