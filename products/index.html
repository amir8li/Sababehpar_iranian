HIGH-PURITY INDUSTRIAL MINERAL POWDERS

Sababehpar Manufactured Product Catalog

Mined directly from our ultra-white Shaghez Aligoodarz marble deposits and micronized via 3 continuous ball mills. Sababehpar Iranian produces stearic acid coated calcium carbonate, ultra-fine uncoated GCC, industrial talc powder, paper grade extenders, and dry-mix construction minerals for B2B manufacturers worldwide.

Coated Grade (Surface Treated) ✨

Stearic Acid Coated Calcium Carbonate

Ultra-white micronized CaCO₃ coated with high-purity stearic acid. Provides hydrophobic surface properties, excellent dispersion, and reduced torque in polymer extrusion.

• Purity & Mesh: 98.5%+ CaCO₃ | 400 to 3500 Mesh (D50: 2µm – 10µm)
• Applications: PVC Pipes & Profiles, PP Masterbatch, Plastic Compounding

Natural Uncoated Grade 💎

Uncoated Micronized Calcium Carbonate

Natural ground marble calcium carbonate with 99.0%+ chemical purity and 98%+ natural brightness. Ideal for water-based coatings, adhesives, and rubber formulations.

• Purity & Mesh: 99.0%+ CaCO₃ | 400 to 3500 Mesh (D50: 1.5µm – 8µm)
• Applications: Emulsion Paints, Adhesives, Rubber, Sealants & Putty
View Uncoated CaCO₃ Specs & PDFs → GCC-U Series
Industrial Talc Grade ⚡

Micronized Industrial Talc Powder

Hydrous magnesium silicate powder engineered with high lamellar platy structure. Enhances stiffness, heat stability, and impact strength in technical polymers.

• Purity & Mesh: 97.0%+ Pure Talc | 800 to 3250 Mesh (D50: 5.0µm ± 0.3)
• Applications: Automotive PP Reinforcement, Anti-Corrosive Paints, Cosmetics
View Talc Specs & Technical PDFs → TALC-M Series
Paper & Packaging Grade 📄

Paper-Grade High-Whiteness CaCO₃

Controlled particle size distribution with high brightness (96%+) and low abrasive index. Replaces costly Titanium Dioxide (TiO₂) in paper filling and coating slurries.

• Brightness & Mesh: 96%+ ISO Brightness | 2500 to 3500 Mesh
• Applications: Alkaline Papermaking, Coated Board, Packaging Sheets
View Paper Grade Specs & PDFs → GCC-P Series
Construction & Dry-Mix Grade 🏗️

Construction & Dry-Mix Mineral Fillers

Coarse and fine ground limestone fillers engineered for dry-mix mortars, tile adhesives, self-leveling floors, and precast concrete formulations.

• Mesh & Purity: 200 to 800 Mesh | 95.0%+ CaCO₃ Content
• Applications: Tile Adhesives, Dry-Mix Mortars, Self-Leveling Screeds, Cement
