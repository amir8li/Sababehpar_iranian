R&D Laboratories & Testing Equipment

In-house laboratory infrastructure equipped for elemental chemical assays and laser particle diffraction analysis.

Malvern Laser Particle Sizer

High-precision laser diffraction analysis measuring D10, D50, and D97 particle distribution curves for uniform batch quality.
