Phase II Quarry Expansion Completed

Sababehpar Iranian is proud to announce the successful completion of our Phase II development at the Shaghez Aligoodarz quarry. This major infrastructure investment has increased our raw white marble extraction capacity by over 30%.

Meeting Global Demand

With the rising global demand for ultra-high purity calcium carbonate across the polymer and paint industries, securing a steady stream of raw material is paramount. The new extraction zones tap into a remarkably pure vein of marble, ensuring that our downstream processing facilities operate at peak efficiency.

We are committed to sustainable mining practices. As part of this expansion, new ecological restoration protocols have been implemented to ensure that extracted zones are rehabilitated concurrently.
