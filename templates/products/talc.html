Talc Powder

Saba Behpar's talc powder, with its natural softness, excellent lubricating properties, and high thermal resistance, is one of the specialized fillers in our product portfolio. Due to its hydrophobicity and lamellar particle structure, this powder plays an important role in improving the physical properties of the paper industry, engineering plastics, rubber industry, and moisture insulations. The talc micronization process in our factory is carried out with high precision so that the layered structure of the particles is not damaged and its optimal properties are preserved. Saba Behpar supplies this product with high purity and completely uniform particle size distribution to industrial markets.
