Ochre Powder

Saba Behpar's ochre powder (natural iron oxide) is recognized as a high-quality mineral pigment due to its exceptional coloring power and high resistance to sunlight and weathering. This natural pigment is widely used in the paint industry, protective coatings, concrete and colored mosaics, and building materials production. The light and thermal stability of this powder prevents color change in products over the long term and creates excellent coverage. The precise processing of this product at Saba Behpar has enabled achieving uniform color shade in all production batches.
