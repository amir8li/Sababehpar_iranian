Environmental Practices & Sustainable Mining

1. Energy Optimization and Management: Due to its energy-intensive nature, the crushing and micronization process at Saba Behpar is carefully managed. By investing in modern equipment and high-efficiency electric motors, we strive to reduce energy consumption in our production unit. Furthermore, our production schedule is designed to distribute workloads intelligently across different hours, preventing excessive strain on the power grid and prioritizing optimal consumption. Our goal is the gradual but continuous reduction of the company's carbon footprint through the smart automation of production line equipment.

2. Waste Management and Zero-Waste Production: Our approach at Saba Behpar is the maximum utilization of extracted minerals. By adopting a "three-tier quality grade production" strategy, we categorize and process all parts of the incoming ore based on their physical and chemical specifications. This precise quality segregation allows us to prevent mineral waste and, instead of discarding, transform every part of the ore into a practical product for various industries. This cycle not only increases economic efficiency but also demonstrates our commitment to the responsible use of natural resources.

3. Advanced Filtration Systems and Air Quality Control: The health of the working environment air and the area surrounding the factory is a vital priority for us. Saba Behpar's production lines are equipped with the most advanced Bag Filter systems, capable of absorbing 99.9% of suspended particles. Through continuous monitoring, these systems prevent dust from escaping into the open air. This measure not only guarantees clean air for local communities but also provides a healthy, respiratory-pollution-free environment for the factory personnel.

4. Economic Development and Local Workforce Empowerment: The development of Saba Behpar is intertwined with the development of the Aligoodarz region. Our primary policy is to prioritize the recruitment and employment of local experts and workforce. We believe that human capital is our most valuable asset; therefore, we implement continuous training programs to upgrade the technical knowledge and specialized skills of our local personnel. Our goal is to create sustainable employment where our regional experts act as the main pillars of production at the highest national standards.

5. Occupational Health and Safety Standards (HSE): At Saba Behpar, safety goes beyond a legal requirement; it is a part of our organizational culture. We have developed and implemented strict protocols for the physical health and safety of all employees. In addition to equipping personnel with advanced personal protective equipment, environmental safety and ventilation systems are designed to neutralize all potential hazards at their inception. Our approach to occupational health is preventive, ensuring that every person who enters the factory in the morning returns home in perfect health at the end of their shift.
