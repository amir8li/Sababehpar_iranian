01 About Sababehpar Iranian

A Trusted Name in Mineral Processing

Sababehpar Iranian Mining Company specializes in the extraction and processing of high-purity calcium carbonate from the premium mines of Shaghaz, Aligoodarz. With state-of-the-art production lines and rigorous quality control, we deliver consistently superior micronized powders to industries across Iran and the region.

Our vertically integrated operation — from mine to finished product — ensures unmatched purity levels and precise particle size distribution tailored to each client's manufacturing specifications.
