## 1. Executive Summary

#### This report analyzes 15 competitor websites in the calcium carbonate (CaCO3) and mineral powder industry - 8 international sites (Omya, Imerys,

#### Sibelco, Nordkalk, ECCF, Huber, BHP, MTI) and 7 Iranian sites (Alvan Powder, Laleh Group, Cacaspian, Tanin Arad, Farayand Powder, Narm

#### Powder, Farapowder). The goal is to identify the optimal sitemap structure for Sababehpar's new B2B corporate website.

#### Key findings: (1) All competitors maintain a blog/knowledge section for SEO, (2) Industry/application-based navigation (not just product-based) is

#### the gold standard for B2B mining companies, (3) Multilingual capability (especially English + Arabic) is critical for export-oriented Iranian companies,

#### (4) Technical datasheets and quality certifications are essential for B2B customer trust-building, (5) Sustainability section has become a requirement

#### for international credibility.

#### Three sitemap options are presented: Minimum Viable (8 pages), Standard B2B (18 pages), and Premium SEO-Optimized (30+ pages). Each option

#### includes page-level content guidance and SEO recommendations.

## 3. International Competitor Deep Dive

#### Omya (omya.com) - Switzerland

#### Gold standard. Omya organizes by: Products, Industries, Sustainability, About, Careers, News, Contact. Industry-first navigation (Paints & Coatings,

#### Plastics, Paper, etc.) allows buyers to self-identify. Deep technical content per industry.

- Sitemap strengths: Industry-first navigation, technical datasheets, global/local structure
- SEO lesson: Industry pages rank for "[industry] calcium carbonate"

#### Imerys (imerys.com) - France

#### Massive site organized by Markets (industries) and Product ranges. 15+ industry pages. Dedicated R&D, Sustainability, Investors sections. Strong

#### brand storytelling.

- Sitemap strengths: Markets > Products structure, dedicated R&D section
- SEO lesson: Comprehensive industry pages, FAQ section

#### ECCF (eccf.ae) - UAE

#### Best mid-size CaCO3 manufacturer site. Clean design with clear CTA paths. Products: natural, coated, aggregates, minerals. Downloadable

#### technical data sheets per product.

- Sitemap strengths: Simple but deep. Login/Register for customers, Careers, FAQ
- SEO lesson: PDF datasheets with SEO titles, clean URL structure

#### Nordkalk (nordkalk.com) - Finland

#### Strong sustainability section. Products organized by type (limestone, quicklime, PCC) AND solutions (agriculture, water treatment, etc.). Sustainability

#### program with 9 focus areas.

- Sitemap strengths: Dual product/solution navigation, sustainability depth
- SEO lesson: Solution-based landing pages, location-specific content

#### MTI (mineralstech.com) - USA

#### Global leader in PCC and bentonite-based products. NYSE-listed with $2.1B revenue. Strong investor hub, dedicated R&D section, and

#### comprehensive sustainability reporting.

- Sitemap strengths: Comprehensive investor hub, strong R&D content
- SEO lesson: Investor-grade content structure, business unit organization

## 11. SEO Strategy & Technical Recommendations

### Technical SEO

- SSL/HTTPS: mandatory for all pages
- Mobile-first responsive design
- Page speed: target under 3s load time
- Clean URL structure: /products/gcc not /product.php?id=
- XML sitemap generation with weekly updates
- robots.txt with proper directives
- Schema markup: Organization, Product, FAQ, Article, BreadcrumbList
- Canonical URLs to prevent duplicate content
- Open Graph tags for social sharing
- Hreflang tags for multilingual
- Google Search Console + Google Analytics 4
- Core Web Vitals: LCP <2.5s, FID <100ms, CLS <0.
- Image optimization: WebP, alt text, descriptive file names
- CDN for global audiences

### Content SEO Strategy

- Blog: at least 2-3 articles per month, 800-1500 words
- Keyword clusters: grouped by product type, industry, and buyer intent
- Internal linking: connect products, industries, and articles
- External linking: to authoritative industry sources
- Evergreen content: "Complete Guide to Calcium Carbonate"
- Long-tail keywords: "best coated CaCO3 for PVC pipe"
- Local SEO: Google Business Profile for Tehran and Aligoodarz
- Backlink strategy: industry directories, exhibition websites
- Infographics and visual content for sharing
- Case studies with real customer results
- FAQ structured data for voice search

### Multilingual SEO Strategy

- Phase 1: Persian + English (immediate, covers 90% of audience)
- Phase 2: Arabic (important for Middle Eastern buyers)
- Phase 3: Turkish (for Turkey market)
- Separate URL per language: /en/, /ar/, /tr/
- Hreflang tag on every page
- Professional translation (not machine) for credibility
- Separate keyword research per language
